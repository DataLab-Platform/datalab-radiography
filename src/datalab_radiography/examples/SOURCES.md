# Sources of the real-data examples

The real-data examples of the plugin are reduced extracts of open datasets, published under the Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International license ([CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)). They are built from the original datasets by `scripts/build_examples.py`. Each image also carries its attribution in its metadata (`plugin.org.datalab.radiography.attribution`).

| Example | File | Source | Changes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Walnut (raw flat-panel radiograph) | `walnut.h5` | H. Der Sarkissian, F. Lucka, M. van Eijnatten, G. Colacicco, S. B. Coban, K. J. Batenburg, « A Cone-Beam X-Ray CT Data Collection Designed for Machine Learning », Sci. Data 6, 215 (2019); CWI FleX-ray Lab, Walnut 1, DOI [10.5281/zenodo.2686726](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.2686726) | First projection of source position 2, first dark and flat images, binned 2 × 2 and cropped |
| Circuit board (laminography, 45° tilt) | `circuit_board.h5` | « Cone-Beam Computed Laminography Dataset of a Keyboard Circuit Board », LUT University, DOI [10.5281/zenodo.17084482](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.17084482) | First projection, binned 2 × 2 |
| Knee phantom (cone-beam CT projection) | `knee_phantom.h5` | « Cone beam computed tomography dataset of a knee phantom », DOI [10.5281/zenodo.12722386](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.12722386) | First projection, binned 2 × 2 |

The acquisition geometries (source-to-detector and source-to-object distances, detector pixel pitch, tube voltage) come from the files of each dataset. The simulated examples (weld, pipe, step wedge, correction campaign) are generated by the plugin.
